# Last Train Home — first-play test

Build: **1.0.0-rc1**,27 September2026. ZIP: `builds/release/LastTrainHome-1.0.0-rc1-macOS.zip`. Extract locally (e.g.Downloads), open Last Train Home.app. Personal local evaluation only; do not publicly share the included Apple system-voice recordings. User explicitly deferred these human tests until after M16 packaging. No completed human results are recorded here.

Use three people who have not read the story/design or played the game. At least one should have little adventure-game experience. Prefer separate macOS accounts so one person's Continue does not become another person's starting point. Reset Game deliberately deletes that account's saved journey; use it only after confirming that the earlier playtest is finished and recorded.

Give testers only these instructions: “Play naturally until you reach an ending. Read at your own pace. If stuck, try the in-game hint. You can pause and change settings. Please say when something confuses you.” Do not tell them the solutions or mystery. Do not show them the design docs or spoiler section below until finished.

## Targets and follow-up

First finish28–35 active minutes; puzzles usually1–3 minutes each. These are targets, not promises. If multiple testers finish much faster/slower, revise content/pacing rather than adding artificial waits. Record outliers and their reasons. An unclear required fact or inaccessible required control is a defect to investigate, not a failed tester.

Across the sessions cover keyboard-only,large text,muted play,reduced motion/flicker and headphone/speaker listening. Do not make a first-time tester repeat the whole story solely to fill a settings matrix; use a separate follow-up session if needed.
